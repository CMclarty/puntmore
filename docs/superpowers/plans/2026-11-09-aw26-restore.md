## Global constraints

- `docs/decisions.md` wins. `CLAUDE.md`, `render/api/AGENTS.md`, `render/frontend/AGENTS.md` apply.
- Render runs nothing in the background for AW 26: no scheduler job, no hook in the Paddy sweep,
  no ingest pipeline, no matching, no result lookup, no OddsPapi request, no photo reading.
- `aw_slips` and `aw_legs` are in the production database with Callum's slips in them. Their two
  models go back with **exactly the archived columns** (names, types, nullability, indexes,
  constraints). No column is added, renamed or dropped.
- `aw_fairs`, `aw_leg_lines`, `value_snapshots`, `value_lines` get **no model**: the tables stay

- One way in for everyone: the page (session) and HubAI (an API client key granted `aw26`) call
  the same routes. There is no ingest-key route for AW 26.
- Names: "Callum", never "Cal"; AW 26 is Callum's, not "the team's". No comment mentions desktop
  scripts, Bookie Bashing, FlashScore or snapshots as a feeder: slips are "recorded by HubAI or
  corrected on the page".
- Frontend: add nothing that was not there before.
- No secrets in any commit.

How the kept code changes:

- `build_slip` takes the record body (`AwSlipRecordIn`) directly; there is no `ExtractedSlip`.
  It does not classify legs or look up fixtures: `kind`, `line`, `side`, `fixture_id` stay NULL
  on new slips.
- `price_slip(slip)` takes no fairs: a leg's fair price is the one on the leg (sent by HubAI or
  typed on the page). Once **every** leg has odds and a fair, the EV is fixed; until then it is
  NULL and `ev_note` says how many legs are waiting. An edit clears and recomputes it, as before.
- `summary(slips, today=None)` loses `ev_by_source` and `value_on_offer`.
- The models lose `line_link` and every property that read a removed module (`ev_by_source`,
  `offered_odds`, `snapshot_at`, `fair_source`). `edge` stays only if the page shows it, computed
  in place.
- The schemas lose the fields those properties fed.
- The page loses the photo upload and anything that showed a removed field. Nothing else changes.
